# Ember Instrument

A precision-instrument theme: warm ember against warm graphite on warm paper.
Built for work where the subject is measurement and judgment — audit, risk,
model governance, regulatory writing. Reads as a calibrated device rather than
a corporate deck.

Derived from a physical object: the ember was sampled from leather under
tungsten light, white-balanced, and cleaned to `#E0662E`.

## Color Palette

- **Ember**: `#E0662E` — the anchor. Marks, rules, fills, large type.
- **Ember Deep**: `#AD4317` — text and links on light grounds (the only ember that may carry body text there).
- **Ember Tint**: `#F58E5C` — text and links on dark grounds.
- **Ink**: `#171A1D` — the record. Warm graphite, never pure black.
- **Muted**: `#5A646E` — secondary text on light; `#A6B0BA` on dark.
- **Paper**: `#F6F3F0` — warm ground. Cold greys break the palette.
- **Night**: `#0E1114` — dark ground.
- **Hairline**: `#E2DAD3` on light; `#263039` on dark.
- **Verdigris**: `#0F5F5A` light / `#4FC0B2` dark — evidence confirmed. Used sparingly and quietly.

## Typography

- **Headers** (*Statement*): system sans, bold, tracking `-0.025em`. The finding.
- **Body** (*Record*): Iowan Old Style / Palatino / Georgia serif. The written argument.
- **Labels** (*Instrument*): monospace, uppercase, tracking `0.16em`. Keys, metadata, anything the instrument itself says.

## Rules

1. Ember `#E0662E` is 3.11:1 on paper. Never set body text in it on a light
   ground — use Ember Deep. This single constraint is what keeps the palette
   from going soft.
2. Ember marks what pulls you. Verdigris marks what was verified. Never swap
   them for visual variety — the semantics are the point.
3. One ember element per view carries the weight. If two things shout, neither
   is a signal.
4. Warm neutrals only. A cold grey next to this ember reads as an error.

## Best Used For

Audit and risk reporting, AI governance material, regulatory and examination
documents, research decks, technical writing that has to look measured rather
than promotional.
