# Reads · *The Development of Metaphysics in Persia*: the harness

Iqbal's doctoral dissertation (Munich, 1907), published by Luzac & Co., London, in 1908 and dedicated to T. W. Arnold. It is public domain. This track reads it over **16 sessions**. Each session puts one section of the book next to the Qur'an and next to Iqbal's own later poetry or prose.

**The reason for the mix:** this is the *young* Iqbal. He was 30, a historian of ideas, and he admired the Persian mystics. Seven years later, in *Asrār-i-Khudī* (1915), he turned against the doctrine that the self should dissolve into God (*fanāʾ*), and argued instead that the self should be strengthened (*khudī*). The poems in this track let you watch him argue with his younger self. The Qur'an passages are the text that both the young Iqbal and the later Iqbal claim to be reading.

## How a session runs (the Amānah order)

1. **Qur'an first.** Read the passage in Abdel Haleem. If you choose, this is the day's fixed portion (*wird*), and it goes on Mīzān's Dīn measure.
2. **The book.** Read the section from the PDF (the `.txt` is only for search and for the AI).
3. **Your line first.** Before you ask any AI, write your own one-line answer to the question in the table.
4. **Then the AI.** Paste your line and the session number into ChatGPT or Jarvis (prompts below). Log the handoff on Mīzān's Amānah card.
5. **Score it.** Put the minutes on ʿIlm in Mīzān. Keep one sentence for the week's carousel or LinkedIn draft.
