Before you upload

Use the square portrait for every profile. The same face in every place is most of what people mean by a consistent brand, and it costs one upload. Pick dark or light for the LinkedIn cover and do not revisit it. Dark matches the GitHub profile; light matches the site. Either is right; switching between them is the only wrong answer.

These files are outputs, not sources. Do not retouch a PNG or edit an SVG by hand — the change will be lost the next time a generator runs. Change the generator in assets/brand/ and re-run it.
