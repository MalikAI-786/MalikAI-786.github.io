02 / The problem—not just the proposed solution

Describe one recurring problem and who it affects. Example: “Managers rebuild the weekly sales report by hand.”

Name a measurable change if you can: hours saved, fewer errors, repeatable training, or a tested expansion decision. Estimates are welcome if labeled.

Describe the baseline or source—not the underlying records. Example: “We estimate six hours each week; we have not timed it yet.”

Example: “We think another location will work; weak repeat visits or staffing constraints would make us reconsider.” It is fine not to know.
