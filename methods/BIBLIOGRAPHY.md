# Bibliography

Every source cited anywhere under `methods/`. A citation marker in a method file
must resolve to an entry here — `tools/audit/invariants.py` checks it, because a
citation that goes nowhere is worse than no citation at all.

Entries are at work level. **No page numbers appear here unless they have been
checked against the source**, and none have yet. Add them as the sources are
read, not from memory.

---

## Method — qualitative design

**Creswell & Poth** — *Qualitative Inquiry and Research Design: Choosing Among
Five Approaches*. Sage. The course text. The five approaches, philosophical
assumptions, and the design framework used throughout this directory.

**Lincoln & Guba (1985)** — *Naturalistic Inquiry*. Sage. Origin of the
trustworthiness criteria — credibility, transferability, dependability,
confirmability — that stand in for validity and reliability.

**Patton** — *Qualitative Research & Evaluation Methods*. Sage. Purposive
sampling typology.

**Miles, Huberman & Saldaña** — *Qualitative Data Analysis: A Methods
Sourcebook*. Sage.

**Saldaña** — *The Coding Manual for Qualitative Researchers*. Sage.

## Method — approaches

**Moustakas (1994)** — *Phenomenological Research Methods*. Sage.

**Glaser & Strauss (1967)** — *The Discovery of Grounded Theory*. Aldine.

**Strauss & Corbin** — *Basics of Qualitative Research*. Sage.

**Charmaz (2006)** — *Constructing Grounded Theory*. Sage. The constructivist
turn — the researcher is part of what is constructed, not a neutral recorder.

**Yin** — *Case Study Research and Applications*. Sage.

**Clandinin & Connelly** — *Narrative Inquiry*. Jossey-Bass.

**Denzin** — *The Research Act*. Origin of the triangulation typology.

## Method — first-order / second-order analysis

**Van Maanen (1979)** — "The Fact of Fiction in Organizational Ethnography."
*Administrative Science Quarterly*. Where the first-order / second-order
distinction comes from.

**Gioia & Chittipeddi (1991)** — "Sensemaking and sensegiving in strategic change
initiation." *Strategic Management Journal*. The longitudinal ethnographic study
of a new university president; insider/outsider pairing; the envisioning,
signalling, re-visioning, energising phases.

**Gioia, Corley & Hamilton (2013)** — "Seeking Qualitative Rigor in Inductive
Research: Notes on the Gioia Methodology." *Organizational Research Methods*.

## Judgment, bias and automation

**Tversky & Kahneman (1974)** — "Judgment under Uncertainty: Heuristics and
Biases." *Science*. Anchoring and adjustment.

**Kahneman, Slovic & Tversky (1982)** — *Judgment Under Uncertainty: Heuristics
and Biases*. Cambridge University Press. The collected volume.

**Parasuraman & Riley (1997)** — "Humans and Automation: Use, Misuse, Disuse,
Abuse." *Human Factors*. The use/misuse framing behind automation bias.

**Skitka, Mosier & Burdick (1999)** — "Does automation bias decision-making?"
*International Journal of Human-Computer Studies*. Omission and commission
errors under automated cueing.

## Language models — agreement and reliability

**Perez et al. (2022)** — "Discovering Language Model Behaviors with
Model-Written Evaluations." Anthropic. Documents sycophancy as a measured
behaviour that scales with model size and RLHF.

**Sharma et al. (2023)** — "Towards Understanding Sycophancy in Language
Models." Anthropic.

> ⚠️ The two entries above are the empirical basis for the sycophancy row in
> `risk/llm-reliance-register.md`. Read them before citing them in submitted
> work — this bibliography records where to look, not what was found.
