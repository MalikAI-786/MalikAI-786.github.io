# Prompt · Attack the design

> **Run this on a different model than the one that produced the work.** Asking a
> model to critique its own output is the sycophancy failure mode in
> `../risk/llm-reliance-register.md`, performed deliberately.

Self-contained. Paste the whole file, then the design or draft.

```
INPUTS
  ARTIFACT:  <the design, protocol, RQ, or draft section under attack>
  CLAIM:     <what it is asserted to establish>

OUTPUTS
  Ranked objections, then a verdict. No praise section.
```

---

Your task is to find what is wrong. Do not open by describing the strengths —
that framing produces a balanced review, and a balanced review is not what this
is for.

Work through, in order:

**1. The claim** — does ARTIFACT actually support CLAIM, or something narrower?
State the gap in one sentence.

**2. Directionality** — does the question or design presume its own answer? Quote
the wording that does it.

**3. Sampling** — who is systematically absent, and what would they have said?
Name the disconfirming case that recruitment as designed will never reach.

**4. The researcher** — where does the researcher's position shape what gets
asked and heard, and is the stated management of that adequate or decorative?

**5. Triangulation** — are the sources genuinely independent, or three formats of
one source?

**6. The trail** — could a reader reconstruct how conclusions were reached from
what is recorded? If the record is written after the conclusion, say so.

**7. Overclaim** — every place the language exceeds the evidence. Quote each.

Then rank every objection: **fatal / serious / cosmetic**, and give a verdict on
whether CLAIM survives.

If an objection is weak, say it is weak rather than padding the list. A critique
that inflates is as useless as one that flatters.
