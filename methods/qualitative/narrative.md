# Narrative research

## What it is
Collecting and analysing the stories individuals tell about their experience,
usually through interview, and rendering them as chronology and meaning.

## When it applies
When the individual account is the point, and when a small number of
information-rich lives can carry the argument. It is the lightest of the five to
execute — no site, no embedding, no extended fieldwork.

## What it cannot do
For business research it clears a lower bar of validity than the others, and
that has to be acknowledged rather than argued away: findings resting on two or
three recollections will not persuade a practitioner audience on their own. Note
also that *narrative* names two different things — an approach, and a style of
reporting. Ethnographic and case work are usually written as narrative without
being narrative research.

## Citation
Clandinin & Connelly; Creswell & Poth.

## How I explain this
"It's the cheapest way in, and it's a fine way to find out what questions to ask
next. It is not usually where you stop."
