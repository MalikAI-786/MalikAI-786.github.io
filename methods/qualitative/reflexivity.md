# Reflexivity

## What it is
The researcher's disclosure and active management of their own position, values
and prior commitments, treated as part of the method rather than as a confession.

## When it applies
Throughout, and especially where the researcher is an insider to the setting.
The techniques are concrete: memoing, journaling, stepping back and asking *why
am I reading it this way*, *what did I not hear*, *who have I not spoken to*,
and pairing with a researcher who does not share the position.

## What it cannot do
It cannot remove bias, and a study claiming it has is making the error it
purports to manage. Disclosure is not neutralisation — naming a position does
not cancel its effect on what got asked. It also relies on the researcher
noticing, which is exactly what unconscious commitments prevent; this is the
argument for scoring predictions against outcomes rather than trusting
introspection. See `DESIGN.md` for that instrument.

## Citation
Lincoln & Guba (1985); Creswell & Poth.

## How I explain this
"In quantitative work I hand you my data and you get my answer. In qualitative
work I am the instrument — so you are entitled to know what the instrument is
made of."
