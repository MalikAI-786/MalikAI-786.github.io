The intoxicant ledger

The staged approach, applied to the thing itself

You asked for both roads — how to be rid of it, and how to use it in a way you actually enjoy. Both are built here. But start with the thing that should be pointed out to an auditor before anything else: the Qur'an does not address intoxicants in one move. It addresses them in three, over years, in a defined sequence. The method is already in the text.

Stage one · acknowledge the benefit
يَسْـَٔلُونَكَ عَنِ ٱلْخَمْرِ وَٱلْمَيْسِرِ ۖ قُلْ فِيهِمَآ إِثْمٌ كَبِيرٌ وَمَنَـٰفِعُ لِلنَّاسِ وَإِثْمُهُمَآ أَكْبَرُ مِن نَّفْعِهِمَا
"They ask you about intoxicants and gambling. Say: in them is great harm, and benefits for people — but their harm is greater than their benefit."
Al-Baqarah 2:219
It concedes the benefit. It does not pretend there is nothing in it for you — it says the ledger nets out against you. Anyone who tells you it does nothing for you has already lost your trust, and they should.
Stage two · protect the spine first
يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ لَا تَقْرَبُوا۟ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَأَنتُمْ سُكَـٰرَىٰ حَتَّىٰ تَعْلَمُوا۟ مَا تَقُولُونَ
"Do not approach prayer while intoxicated, until you know what you are saying."
An-Nisāʾ 4:43
Not "stop" — ring-fence it. Carve out the hours the spine occupies. In practice that is a large and awkward share of the waking day, which is exactly the point: the constraint does the arguing for you. This instrument computes those windows and flags collisions automatically.
Stage three · the clean break
إِنَّمَا ٱلْخَمْرُ وَٱلْمَيْسِرُ ... رِجْسٌ مِّنْ عَمَلِ ٱلشَّيْطَـٰنِ فَٱجْتَنِبُوهُ لَعَلَّكُمْ تُفْلِحُونَ
"Intoxicants and gambling … are defilement from the work of Satan, so avoid it, that you may be successful."
Al-Māʾidah 5:90
Only at the end, after the ground had been prepared for years, does the instruction become total. A staged taper is not a compromise with the text. It is the text's own method.
Two things stated plainly, so nothing here is smuggled past you. On the fiqh: the majority position treats anything that intoxicates or clouds the mind — cannabis explicitly included in the classical discussions from Ibn Taymiyyah onward — under the same ruling as khamr. This page does not have standing to issue a ruling and does not attempt one; take that question to a scholar you trust. On the medicine: I am not a clinician and none of this is medical advice. If ADD is undiagnosed or untreated, an actual evaluation will outperform every mechanic on this page combined, and it is the single highest-return action available to you. If use is daily and heavy, stopping abruptly can produce a genuinely rough week — irritability, sleep disruption, appetite loss — which is a reason to taper with support, not a reason to keep going.

Ledger metrics · 30 days

Trigger distribution — what is actually being medicated

The one to watch is avoidance. Reaching for it when bored is a habit problem and habits are tractable. Reaching for it at the exact moment a hard, unstructured, high-stakes task comes into view — the chapter, the exam prep, the difficult email — is an escape from an aversive state, and that is the mechanism that costs you the doctorate rather than the evening. If avoidance dominates this chart, the intervention is not willpower; it is making the task smaller until it stops triggering the escape.

Displacement — what it took, priced honestly

Sleep is the compounding one. Cannabis reliably shortens the time to fall asleep and reliably suppresses REM; you wake with the subjective sense of having slept and the cognitive profile of someone who has not. For a brain already short on executive fuel, that is a tax levied the next morning, on the exact faculty you need most — which then makes the next evening's urge stronger. That is a loop, and loops are what you get paid to find.
