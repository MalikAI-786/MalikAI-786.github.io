How the model changed, and why.

Every entry names the feedback that caused it, by participant code, and the commit that carried it. A change with no feedback behind it is marked as the owner’s own. If something you sent is not here, it was read and not adopted — the entry says so, and says why.

How to read a packet

A packet is plain text between MIZAN-LAB-PACKET and END-PACKET. Inside is JSON: your code, your onboarding answers (minus the handle and the commitment line), per-day computed scores, closures, fit ratings, and the three feedback boxes verbatim. Each night the owner’s assistant reads any packet that has arrived, writes an entry here, changes the instrument where the case is made, and drafts a note back to the participant describing what was and was not adopted. Nothing is sent automatically; the owner sends the note.
