Mīzān. lab

Participant edition

You are being asked to test an instrument, not to be measured by it.

Mīzān scores a day against seven measures drawn from the Qur’ān, and reads the result through Iqbal’s three stages of khudī — selfhood. The owner built it for himself. This copy exists so that someone else can run it on their own days and tell us where the model is wrong. Your scores stay on this device. The only thing that ever leaves is a feedback packet you build and send yourself, and you can read every byte of it before you do.

Five questions, once

A forecast. In two weeks the sheet will tell you whether you were right — that gap is one of the things the model is for.
Self-placement. The instrument will compute its own after fourteen days, from gates it publishes. The two rarely agree at first.

Where feedback goes

If you arrived by a link they sent, these may already be filled in from that link. This page never sends anything itself — it opens your mail app with the packet in the body, and you press send.

What leaves this device

Only when you send a packet, and only this: a participant code the page generates (not your name), your four answers above except the handle and the commitment line, each day’s computed scores and whether you closed it, your fit ratings, and whatever you type in the three feedback boxes. Not the One Thing, not the closing line, not the commitment.

Iṭāʿat

Days 1–10. Three measures only: prayer, body, action. Form before judgment. The other four are switched off and the page says so.

Ẓabt-e-nafs

Days 11–20. Restraint and attention switch on. The week that decides the rest.

ʿIshq → Niyābat

Days 21–40. All seven, then one external requirement: something leaves your hands each week.
Iqbal’s three-stage structure is from Asrār-i-Khudī. The measures cite sūrah:āyah so they can be checked against a muṣḥaf. The rubrics, weights, phases and gates are constructed and carry no authority beyond their usefulness — which is exactly what you are here to test.

Every 0–3 has four written definitions; the one you chose is shown under the control. If two definitions feel indistinguishable on a real day, that is a finding — put it in the feedback box.
