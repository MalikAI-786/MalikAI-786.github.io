The body is the instrument of the self, not its opponent

Iqbal will not let you split these. In the Reconstruction the ego is a unity — the body is not a cage the soul is serving time in, it is the self's means of acting in the world. A weak instrument is a ceiling on khudī, not a separate department of life. Which is why this module is wired directly into the ʿAmal measure: it will show you, from your own logged days, what training and sleep are actually worth in output.

وَزَادَهُۥ بَسْطَةً فِى ٱلْعِلْمِ وَٱلْجِسْمِ
"And He increased him abundantly in knowledge and physical stature."
Al-Baqarah 2:247 · why Ṭālūt was chosen to lead
When the criterion for command is given explicitly in the Qur'an, it is ʿilm and jism together — the learning and the frame. Not one standing in for the other. You are already spending years on the first half.
"The strong believer is more beloved to Allah than the weak believer — though there is good in both."
ṢAḤĪḤ MUSLIM
Note the second clause. The tradition refuses to write off the weak believer, and it still states a preference. That is the exact register this module is written in: no contempt, and no pretending the two are equivalent.
وَكُلُوا۟ وَٱشْرَبُوا۟ وَلَا تُسْرِفُوٓا۟ ۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلْمُسْرِفِينَ

And the prophetic gloss that makes it operational: no vessel is filled worse than a stomach — a third for food, a third for drink, a third for breath (Tirmidhī). A ratio, not a calorie count. That distinction is the whole design of the food panel below.

The rotation

Built from six years of your own session log — and corrected for what that log shows you have been avoiding.
Why legs land twice. In most long-running training logs the split that gets skipped is legs — it is the one that hurts, the one with no mirror payoff, and the one easiest to swap for another push day. It is also where four of the five Best-50 standards live: the pistol squat, the front split, the burpee test, and the hip compression the flag needs. When the gap and the goal are the same body part, the schedule has to force the issue rather than leave it to preference. So legs sit on the two days with the most room, and every leg day carries one skill progression instead of being general work. The Balance line in the coach's read below computes your actual split from your logged sessions — that is the number to argue with, not this paragraph.

Why this is four rules and not a calorie tracker

Calorie logging asks for a sustained, low-reward, high-precision data-entry habit — which is close to the worst possible ask for the wiring described two sections up. It works for about eleven days and then the app goes unopened, and the failure gets filed under willpower. Four binary rules survive because each one is a single decision made once, not a running tally.

Rule 01
Protein floor
Roughly a gram per pound of goal bodyweight — set the figure in the settings card below. Hit the floor and the rest largely takes care of itself — it is the one macro that is genuinely hard to overshoot and easy to undershoot.
Rule 02
Eating window
A start and an end. The end matters more: it protects sleep, and sleep is the input to everything else on this page.
Rule 03
The thirds
Stop at two-thirds. The tradition gave you a ratio precisely because it does not require a scale, a label, or arithmetic at the table.
Rule 04
Nothing after ʿIshāʾ
The window closes with the last prayer. A rule with an existing external clock is a rule you will actually keep — same logic as ṣalāh carrying the heaviest weight.
The sunnah fast, scheduled around your actual week. Monday and Thursday are the traditional voluntary fasts — but Thursday is now a training day, and a 5pm session at the end of a fast is a bad trade in summer when Maghrib sits near 8pm. Take Monday as the standing fast: it is a non-training day, it follows the weekend's eating, and it costs you nothing in the gym. Add Thursday only in the winter months, when Maghrib arrives before the session ends and you are breaking the fast mid-workout rather than after it.

Best 50

The standards

Composite
—

These are the ones you wrote down yourself. What was missing was a ladder — a skill you cannot currently do is not a goal, it is a wish, until it is broken into levels with a pass condition at each one. Set your honest level; the next drill appears. Test on the first Saturday of the month and only then — testing constantly is how you convert training days into ego days.

Why these and not a barbell number. Every one of them is bodyweight, joint-forgiving, and unfakeable — you can rationalise a squat number by shortening the range, but you cannot slightly do a pistol squat, or slightly hang a strict pull-up. They also skew toward the qualities that decline first and matter most later: unilateral strength, hip and hamstring range, wrist and shoulder integrity, grip and pulling capacity, and the ability to hold a position under tension. That is the difference between being strong at fifty and being strong for fifty.
