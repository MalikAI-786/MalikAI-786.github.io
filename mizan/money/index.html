Money

What comes in, what goes out, and what is owed.

The audit question asked of money: not "am I careful" but does the record match the story. Most people can say roughly what they earn. Very few can list their recurring bills from memory, or say what this month's mortgage payment cost in interest. This page computes both from the statements themselves.

“O you who believe, when you contract a debt for a fixed term, write it down.”
Al-Baqarah 2:282 · working translation

Import a statement

From your bank or card website: Download transactions as CSV, or OFX / QFX (Quicken format) if offered.
Read on this device only. Duplicates are skipped, so overlapping downloads are safe. Transfers between your own accounts are left out of spending.

Where this data lives

In this browser only, under its own storage key, separate from the rest of Mīzān. Nothing is uploaded and this page makes no network calls. The weekly Mīzān export does not include it — use Back up below for that.
Live bank sync is stage 2: a private backend you own, connected through Plaid. Until then, nothing here can reach your bank, and your bank cannot reach it.
