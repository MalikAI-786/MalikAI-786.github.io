The seven measures

One thing

A list of twelve is a list of zero. One target, named the night before, is the single highest-yield accommodation for an attention-regulation problem — it removes the decision, which is the part that costs you.

The reference line

What is being measured, and on whose authority

Seven measures, each anchored to a verse and each carrying a fixed weight. Scoring is 0–3 against a written rubric, because a scale without definitions is a mood ring. References are given by sūrah:āyah so you can verify every one against a muṣḥaf — renderings below are working translations, not a substitute for the Arabic.

وَٱلسَّمَآءَ رَفَعَهَا وَوَضَعَ ٱلْمِيزَانَ ◆ أَلَّا تَطْغَوْا۟ فِى ٱلْمِيزَانِ ◆ وَأَقِيمُوا۟ ٱلْوَزْنَ بِٱلْقِسْطِ وَلَا تُخْسِرُوا۟ ٱلْمِيزَانَ
"And the heaven He raised, and He set the balance — that you not transgress within the balance. So establish weight in justice, and do not fall short in the balance."

Weighting

Three faculties

Yaqīn · conviction · ṣalāh + dhikr
The measures with a fixed form. Iqbal's point is that form precedes feeling: the discipline installs the certainty, not the reverse.
ʿAmal · action · work + attention + body
Khudī is proved in output. "And that there is nothing for man except what he strives for" — 53:39.
ʿIshq · love · iḥsān + restraint
Restraint sits under love deliberately. In Iqbal, zabt is not repression; it is what love pays. Restraint without love produces a brittle self that snaps.

Maturity model

The Khudī ladder

In Asrār-i-Khudī (1915) Iqbal sets out three stages in the education of the self: obedience to the law, then self-control, then vicegerency — the self strong enough to be entrusted with the world. It maps almost exactly onto the Qur'an's three namings of the nafs. It also maps onto something you already know how to read: a control maturity rating, computed on evidence, revisable downward.

The Qur'an's three selves

إِنَّ ٱلنَّفْسَ لَأَمَّارَةٌۢ بِٱلسُّوٓءِ إِلَّا مَا رَحِمَ رَبِّىٓ
"Indeed the self is a persistent enjoiner of evil, except those on which my Lord has mercy."
Yūsuf 12:53 · an-nafs al-ammārah — the self that commands
وَلَآ أُقْسِمُ بِٱلنَّفْسِ ٱللَّوَّامَةِ
"And I swear by the self-reproaching soul."
Al-Qiyāmah 75:2 · an-nafs al-lawwāmah — the self that argues back
يَـٰٓأَيَّتُهَا ٱلنَّفْسُ ٱلْمُطْمَئِنَّةُ ٱرْجِعِىٓ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكِ رَاضِيَةً مَّرْضِيَّةً
"O soul at peace, return to your Lord, well-pleased and well-pleasing."
Al-Fajr 89:27–28 · an-nafs al-muṭma'innah — the settled self
Note the middle stage. The self-reproaching soul is sworn by — the internal argument is not a symptom of failure, it is the sound of the second stage working. Most people quit there because they mistake the noise for regression.

"Raise the self so high that, before each decree, God Himself asks the servant: tell Me, what is it that you want."

یقین محکم، عمل پیہم، محبت فاتحِ عالم

Yaqīn-e-muḥkam, ʿamal-e-paiham, moḥabbat fātiḥ-e-ʿālam / Jihād-e-zindagānī meñ haiñ ye mardoñ kī shamshīreñ
"Firm conviction, ceaseless action, love that conquers the world — in the struggle of life, these are a man's swords."
Bāl-e-Jibrīl · the three faculties this instrument scores
What Iqbal is not saying. Khudī is not ego in the English sense and not self-esteem. It is selfhood — a centre of will that becomes more real the more it acts, resists dissolution, and takes responsibility. In the Reconstruction lectures he argues the self is sharpened by contact with resistant reality, not by escaping it. Which is why his sharpest polemic is against inherited quietism: the trance, the withdrawal, the pleasant dissolving of the self into a mood. He would have had a specific and unkind word for anything that softens the edges of the self on a Tuesday night.

Attention is an amānah

Working with the wiring, not against it

An attention-regulation difference is not a character defect and this instrument does not treat it as one. What it does assume: your working memory is unreliable as a storage device, your motivation is interest-driven rather than importance-driven, and transitions cost you more than the tasks do. Every mechanic below is built around those three facts.

The laghw filter

قَدْ أَفْلَحَ ٱلْمُؤْمِنُونَ ◆ ٱلَّذِينَ هُمْ فِى صَلَاتِهِمْ خَـٰشِعُونَ ◆ وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمْ عَنِ ٱللَّغْوِ مُعْرِضُونَ
"Successful are the believers — those who are humbly submissive in their prayer, and those who turn away from what is vain."
Al-Mu'minūn 23:1–3 · laghw: the empty, the idle, the noise

Note the ordering: focus in prayer, then turning from noise. Attention is treated as one faculty with one budget. The feed is not competing with your leisure — it is competing with your khushūʿ, and it is winning because it was engineered to.

Hyperfocus harvest

The same wiring that will not produce twenty minutes on a form will produce five unbroken hours on something that has hooked it. That is not a deficit, it is an unaimed asset — and it is very close to what Iqbal means by ʿishq: intensity with a direction. The discipline is not manufacturing focus. It is pointing it at the doctorate on Sunday rather than at a rabbit hole on Sunday, and logging where it actually landed so the pattern becomes visible.

Badan · the mount

The body is the instrument of the self, not its opponent

Iqbal will not let you split these. In the Reconstruction the ego is a unity — the body is not a cage the soul is serving time in, it is the self's means of acting in the world. A weak instrument is a ceiling on khudī, not a separate department of life. Which is why this module is wired directly into the ʿAmal measure: it will show you, from your own logged days, what training and sleep are actually worth in output.

Why this is four rules and not a calorie tracker

Calorie logging asks for a sustained, low-reward, high-precision data-entry habit — which is close to the worst possible ask for the wiring described two sections up. It works for about eleven days and then the app goes unopened, and the failure gets filed under willpower. Four binary rules survive because each one is a single decision made once, not a running tally.

Rule 01
Protein floor
Roughly a gram per pound of goal bodyweight. At 171 lb that is a floor near 150 g. Hit the floor and the rest largely takes care of itself — it is the one macro that is genuinely hard to overshoot and easy to undershoot.
Rule 02
Eating window
A start and an end. The end matters more: it protects sleep, and sleep is the input to everything else on this page.
Rule 03
The thirds
Stop at two-thirds. The tradition gave you a ratio precisely because it does not require a scale, a label, or arithmetic at the table.
Rule 04
Nothing after ʿIshāʾ
The window closes with the last prayer. A rule with an existing external clock is a rule you will actually keep — same logic as ṣalāh carrying the heaviest weight.
The sunnah fast, scheduled around your actual week. Monday and Thursday are the traditional voluntary fasts — but Thursday is now a training day, and a 5pm session at the end of a fast is a bad trade in summer when Maghrib sits near 8pm. Take Monday as the standing fast: it is a non-training day, it follows the weekend's eating, and it costs you nothing in the gym. Add Thursday only in the winter months, when Maghrib arrives before the session ends and you are breaking the fast mid-workout rather than after it.

These are the five you wrote down yourself. What was missing was a ladder — a skill you cannot currently do is not a goal, it is a wish, until it is broken into levels with a pass condition at each one. Set your honest level; the next drill appears. Test on the first Saturday of the month and only then — testing constantly is how you convert training days into ego days.

Why these five and not a barbell number. That is the difference between being strong at fifty and being strong for fifty.

The intoxicant ledger

The staged approach, applied to the thing itself

You asked for both roads — how to be rid of it, and how to use it in a way you actually enjoy. Both are built here. But start with the thing that should be pointed out to an auditor before anything else: the Qur'an does not address intoxicants in one move. It addresses them in three, over years, in a defined sequence. The method is already in the text.

Stage one · acknowledge the benefit
يَسْـَٔلُونَكَ عَنِ ٱلْخَمْرِ وَٱلْمَيْسِرِ ۖ قُلْ فِيهِمَآ إِثْمٌ كَبِيرٌ وَمَنَـٰفِعُ لِلنَّاسِ وَإِثْمُهُمَآ أَكْبَرُ مِن نَّفْعِهِمَا
"They ask you about intoxicants and gambling. Say: in them is great harm, and benefits for people — but their harm is greater than their benefit."
Al-Baqarah 2:219
It concedes the benefit. It does not pretend there is nothing in it for you — it says the ledger nets out against you. Anyone who tells you it does nothing for you has already lost your trust, and they should.
Stage two · protect the spine first
يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ لَا تَقْرَبُوا۟ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَأَنتُمْ سُكَـٰرَىٰ حَتَّىٰ تَعْلَمُوا۟ مَا تَقُولُونَ
"Do not approach prayer while intoxicated, until you know what you are saying."
An-Nisāʾ 4:43
Not "stop" — ring-fence it. Carve out the hours the spine occupies. In practice that is a large and awkward share of the waking day, which is exactly the point: the constraint does the arguing for you. This instrument computes those windows and flags collisions automatically.
Stage three · the clean break
إِنَّمَا ٱلْخَمْرُ وَٱلْمَيْسِرُ ... رِجْسٌ مِّنْ عَمَلِ ٱلشَّيْطَـٰنِ فَٱجْتَنِبُوهُ لَعَلَّكُمْ تُفْلِحُونَ
"Intoxicants and gambling … are defilement from the work of Satan, so avoid it, that you may be successful."
Al-Māʾidah 5:90
Only at the end, after the ground had been prepared for years, does the instruction become total. A staged taper is not a compromise with the text. It is the text's own method.
Two things stated plainly, so nothing here is smuggled past you. On the fiqh: the majority position treats anything that intoxicates or clouds the mind — cannabis explicitly included in the classical discussions from Ibn Taymiyyah onward — under the same ruling as khamr. This page does not have standing to issue a ruling and does not attempt one; take that question to a scholar you trust. On the medicine: I am not a clinician and none of this is medical advice. If ADD is undiagnosed or untreated, an actual evaluation will outperform every mechanic on this page combined, and it is the single highest-return action available to you. If use is daily and heavy, stopping abruptly can produce a genuinely rough week — irritability, sleep disruption, appetite loss — which is a reason to taper with support, not a reason to keep going.

Ledger metrics · 30 days

Trigger distribution — what is actually being medicated

The one to watch is avoidance. Reaching for it when bored is a habit problem and habits are tractable. Reaching for it at the exact moment a hard, unstructured, high-stakes task comes into view — the chapter, the exam prep, the difficult email — is an escape from an aversive state, and that is the mechanism that costs you the doctorate rather than the evening. If avoidance dominates this chart, the intervention is not willpower; it is making the task smaller until it stops triggering the escape.

Displacement — what it took, priced honestly

Sleep is the compounding one. Cannabis reliably shortens the time to fall asleep and reliably suppresses REM; you wake with the subjective sense of having slept and the cognitive profile of someone who has not. For a brain already short on executive fuel, that is a tax levied the next morning, on the exact faculty you need most — which then makes the next evening's urge stronger. That is a loop, and loops are what you get paid to find.

Self-calibration — forecast vs actual

Arbaʿīn · forty days

The path, phased

Forty is the classical interval — the period of Mūsā's appointment (2:51, 7:142), the age of prophethood, the traditional length of a spiritual discipline. It is also, conveniently, long enough for a behaviour to stop requiring negotiation. Four phases of ten days. Each phase adds; nothing is dropped. The instrument only holds you to the measures the current phase has switched on — scoring all seven from day one is how this fails in week two.

Why this order and not another

Form before feeling. Phase one changes almost nothing except that it starts measuring. An auditor does not remediate during the walkthrough — you take the baseline first, or you will never know what moved. Ten days of honest logging with zero moralising is not a soft start, it is the control period, and skipping it is why previous attempts had nothing to point at.
Restraint second, not first. Phase two tightens once, on one thing: the intoxicant does not precede the day's ʿamal. Not "quit" — reorder. Everything you want from it is still available at the end of the day; what is removed is its function as an escape hatch, which is the only function that is actually costing you.
Love third, because restraint alone goes brittle. Iqbal is emphatic on this and it is the part every self-improvement scheme misses. A self built only from prohibition is thin and it snaps under load. Phase three adds the two measures that put something in — the night portion, and one concrete act toward another person daily. This is the phase that decides whether the other thirty days survive.
Output last, because that is the whole point. Niyābat is not a feeling of closeness. It is being trusted with something. Phase four requires you to ship — a chapter section, a lecture built, a tool published, a comment letter filed. Khudī is proved in creation. A self-audit that produces only a well-audited self has failed its own test.

When it breaks

لَا تَقْنَطُوا۟ مِن رَّحْمَةِ ٱللَّهِ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَغْفِرُ ٱلذُّنُوبَ جَمِيعًا
"Do not despair of the mercy of God. Indeed God forgives all sins."
Az-Zumar 39:53
It will break. The failure mode is not the broken day — it is the four unlogged days after it, when the instrument goes dark precisely when it would have been most useful. Log the bad day. Score it honestly. Close it. A logged relapse is a data point in a system that is still running. An abandoned week is a system that stopped, and stopped systems are the only ones that never recover.

Provenance

Qur'anic citations are given by sūrah:āyah throughout so that every one can be checked against a muṣḥaf. The Arabic is set for reading, not for recitation reference — verify before quoting publicly. English renderings are working translations aimed at conveying sense; where a rendering carries interpretive weight the reference is there so you can go behind it.
Iqbal is quoted where the attribution is established — the two couplets under the Khudī ladder are from Bāl-e-Jibrīl. The three-stage structure of the education of khudī (iṭāʿat, ẓabt-e-nafs, niyābat-e-ilāhī) is from Asrār-i-Khudī. Statements about his broader position — the self sharpened by resistant reality, the polemic against quietism — are paraphrase of the argument in The Reconstruction of Religious Thought in Islam (1930), not quotation.
Scoring rubrics, weights, thresholds and phase design are constructed for this instrument. They are not derived from any classical source and carry no authority beyond their usefulness. They are declared rather than hidden so you can argue with them, which you should.
Claims about sleep, cannabis and attention reflect the general direction of the research literature as of the model's training, stated without citation. Treat them as prompts to verify, not as evidence. This page is not medical, psychiatric or religious advice.

A closing note on the method

The reason this is built as an audit instrument rather than a habit tracker is that habit trackers measure compliance, and compliance is not what you are after. An audit asks a harder question: is the control designed correctly, and does it operate effectively? A day where you prayed all five and shipped nothing passed one test and failed the other. The index is built to make that visible instead of letting a high score on the easy measures cover a structural failure on the hard ones.

The second reason: you are the population of one that your own research is about. You study how professional judgment degrades when an assistant makes an answer feel more certain than the evidence supports. The forecast field is that same experiment run on yourself, with you as both the model and the reviewer. If your morning forecast is systematically optimistic — and it will be — you have just measured, on your own behaviour, the precise failure mode you are writing a dissertation about.
