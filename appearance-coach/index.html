What is this idea?

A personal appearance coach would start with a photo, explain what works and suggest three improvements. Later, it could show an illustrative preview and remember the preferences you choose to save.

This working demo lets you try the journey with a local photo and suggestions based on your answers. Live photo analysis, scores, generated previews, saved style memory and shopping are future work.
