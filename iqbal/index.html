Three pieces, one argument

  1. Lab pe āti hai duʿā ban ke tamannā merī
    Bachche kī Duʿā · Bāng-e-Darā · 1902
    The child's prayer every one of us learned first. Read slowly, it is not a school song — it is a request to be made useful.
  2. Shikwa — the complaint
    Bāng-e-Darā · 1909 · selected stanzas
    A believer argues with God and is not struck down for it. The daring of the poem is the point.
  3. Jawāb-e-Shikwa — the answer
    Bāng-e-Darā · 1913 · selected stanzas
    The reply turns the complaint back on the complainant: the loss was not God's absence but ours.
