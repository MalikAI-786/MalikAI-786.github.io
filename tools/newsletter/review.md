# The gate

A draft is *proposed* content. It is not publishable until it clears this gate.
Nothing skips it — not a short issue, not a good one, not one that is late.

Run it in order. **Section A is pass/fail: one failure stops the draft.**
Section B is quality: a draft can ship with a B-list weakness noted, but three
or more means it goes back.

Who runs it: the **newsletter-editor** agent drafts and self-checks; the
**malik-record** agent verifies every claim in A2. Yasir gives the final yes.
An agent never clears its own draft to publish.

---

## A · Blocking. One failure and it does not ship.

**A1 · Nothing invented.** Every fact about him is on the whitelist in
`voice.md`. No date, title, employer, credential, metric or finding that is not
sourced. No approximated year. No rounded-up number.

**A2 · Facts verified against the record.** `malik-record` has checked each
claim and returned a source for it. Where the record conflicts with the public
site — the regulator line is the live example — the corrected version is used
and the conflict is noted.

**A3 · Research not advanced.** The completed study reads as **feasibility, not
validation**, everywhere it appears. The AI-and-judgment work reads as **in
development** — a question, never a result. Nothing is described as
peer-reviewed, published or replicated without a citation.

**A4 · Citations exact and current.** Every framework reference checked against
the live text, not memory. SR 26-2 superseded SR 11-7 in April 2026. A wrong
citation in front of this audience is the most expensive error available.

**A5 · No confidential or attributable material.** No examination material, no
client specifics, no named institution as the site of a failure unless already
public. No invented quotes, clients or composite anecdotes presented as real.
Hypotheticals say they are hypothetical.

**A6 · No participant data.** IRB-25-0462. Nothing from the study — not a
response, not a quote, not a paraphrase.

**A7 · No account-level financial detail.** No real positions, sizes, balances,
account numbers or wallet addresses. The SPX instrument is referenceable *with*
its educational-use disclaimer intact; his real money is not, pending an
explicit disclosure decision.

**A8 · Disclosure intact.** The drafting-assistance line is present and
unsoftened. Educational-use disclaimers, where the issue touches markets or the
research instrument, are present and unsoftened.

---

## B · Quality. Three or more weaknesses and it goes back.

**B1 · It opens on something that happened.** A document, a meeting, a
walkthrough, a line in a framework. Not a definition, not a trend, not "in
today's landscape." Concrete before abstract.

**B2 · The argument is carried on evidence.** Every load-bearing claim has
something behind it. "Studies show" and "experts agree" are the unevidenced
claims this newsletter exists to object to — cite the thing or cut the
sentence.

**B3 · A practitioner knows what to do differently.** Someone in internal audit
or model risk can act on it on Monday. If the takeaway is "be careful," it has
not landed.

**B4 · It sounds like him.** Short declaratives, weight at the end. A claim,
then what it costs. First person, willing to be the one who was wrong. No
LinkedIn cadence, no triads of abstractions, no throat-clearing, no selling.

**B5 · It earns its length.** 1,200–1,800 words unless the material genuinely
demands more. Cut anything that survives only because it was written.

**B6 · It does not land too cleanly.** Where the honest position is unsettled,
it closes on the question. He would rather be corrected than agreed with, and a
draft that resolves everything has usually overclaimed something.

**B7 · Mechanics.** American spelling. No serial comma. Numbers spelled out in
prose, figures for money and percentages.

---

## Recording the result

Every run writes a verdict — in the Notion pipeline row, and in the handback
note at the bottom of the draft:

```
GATE: PASS · A1-A8 clear · B: B5 noted (runs long at 2,050 — cut the
      framework recap in §3)
```

or

```
GATE: FAIL · A4 — SR 26-2 effective date not verified against the live text
```

A FAIL names the specific check. "Needs work" is not a verdict.

## Why the gate exists

His own record, 3 August 2026: *the bottleneck is the send step, not
discovery.* Ten runs of good output, near-zero submissions. An open-ended
"is this good enough?" has no answer, so nothing ever ships. A gate with named
checks has an answer, and the answer arrives in minutes.

The gate exists to let things through.
