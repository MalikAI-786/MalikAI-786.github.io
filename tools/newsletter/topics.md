# Issue queue

Candidates for Proof Over Promise, in the order I'd ship them. Every one is
built from material **already public on the site** — no new facts needed, so
none of them is blocked on research. Pick one, open it, run the drafter.

Working cadence: **fortnightly**. The site promises "no schedule I would not
keep," so the schedule should be one that survives a bad week.

---

## 01 · The three links — *Judgment*

The chain already written out on the site: the output becomes the anchor, the
model confirms rather than challenges, conclusions converge on each other
rather than on evidence. Stated plainly, that is a control problem, not a
technology problem — a system that agrees with you, feeding a record that
remembers the agreement.

**Why first:** it is the argument the whole newsletter rests on, it is already
written in his own words, and it needs no fact that isn't public.
**Source:** `index.html` → Research → Current direction.

## 02 · Six in one hundred thousand — *Practice*

The qualifying study screened 330,000 panel participants and found roughly 20
eligible. It was reframed as a feasibility study, and the constraint became the
contribution: specialist professional populations cannot be reached through
standard research infrastructure at any price.

**Why it works:** it is a public account of something that did not go to plan,
which is exactly what the Practice thread promises. A record that only contains
successes is not a record.
**Careful:** feasibility, **not** validation. Do not let the draft upgrade it.
**Source:** `index.html` → Research → What the study actually found.

## 03 · SR 11-7 is gone — *Governance*

SR 26-2 superseded SR 11-7 in April 2026. What actually changed, what carried
over, and which judgment calls the new guidance hands back to you without
saying so.

**Why it works:** highest search intent of anything on the list, and directly
in the seat he sits in.
**Needs:** a careful read of the current text before drafting. This is the one
topic where a wrong citation is expensive.

## 04 · The control that was operating but not effective — *Evidence*

The distinction that runs through fifteen years of findings: a control that
exists on paper and cannot be evidenced. Written as what he asks for, in what
order, and why "we have a policy" ends the conversation badly.

**Why it works:** it is the newsletter's founding observation, told as method
rather than complaint.
**Source:** `newsletter.html` → Why I write this.

## 05 · Nobody documents the moment they stopped thinking — *Ethics*

You cannot examine your way out of deference. There is no finding to write and
nothing for a watchdog to bite, so the question becomes how you teach judgment
instead — using the same discipline.

**Why it works:** the most distinctive thing on the site and the least like
anything else in this space. Also the natural bridge to the teaching ambition.
**Source:** `index.html` → Focus → Audit ethics and governance.
