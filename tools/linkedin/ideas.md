# Idea bank

Everything, unranked. Adding costs one line. Most of these are nothing, and
that is the point — an inbox that only accepts good ideas stops receiving.

Format, loosely: a line stating the **finding**, not the topic. "Anchoring in
audit" is a topic and will sit here forever. "The prior year's number is in the
workpaper before the fieldwork starts, so the fieldwork is a confirmation
exercise" is a finding and can be drafted tomorrow.

Mark promoted ideas `→ queue/NNN`. Strike through the ones that died, and leave
them visible; a struck line is a record of a judgment and occasionally gets
revived.

**Public file.** No client names, no employer-internal detail, no participant
data, nothing personal. See `README.md`.

## Measures series

Full architecture in `.claude/skills/linkedin/references/series-measures.md`.
Listed here only so the bank is one surface.

  one. Every institution knows the difference in cost and most still wait.
  → `queue/001`
- **M2 Su'āl** — Iqbal: anything received rather than earned weakens the
  receiver. The answer taken from an assistant without being checked is the

  reason. Test: could you rebuild it from its purpose? → `queue/005`

  contingent on approval means the controls are already someone else's.
  → `queue/007`
- **M5 Serial time vs duration** — a streak resets and makes a claim about the

  because it will never be remediated.
- **M7 Ghulāmī** — action determined elsewhere, "including the ones you have
  agreed to and the ones that pay well". The control function that reports to
  what it examines.
- **M8 Taqdīr** — determinism is what a weak ego calls its situation. The
  governance version: it is the system, we cannot change it.
- **M9 Kasb-e-ḥalāl** — the falcon does not scavenge. What you may present as

- **M10 Himmat** — writing the finding that costs you the relationship.
- **M11 Taskhīr** — the auditor who builds rather than accommodates.
- **M12 Bekhudī** — Iqbal's correction to his own poem, applied to the

## The working record

Things he actually did, built, or found. These carry the series — a feed of
only philosophy reads as a devotional.

- The RAG assistant shipped into a live audit function. The interesting part is
  not that it worked; it is what had to be true about the control environment
  before it was allowed to. → `queue/002`
- Building Mīzān: why the score is computed rather than entered, and what
  changes about a measurement when you cannot award yourself the rating.
  → `queue/004`
- The instrument shows streaks but never lets them gate the ladder. Why a
  metric that can be broken by one bad Thursday is a claim about the calendar.
- Writing an AI governance framework for an internal governance team: the
  section that was hardest was human-in-the-loop, because "a human reviews it"
  is not a control until you can say what would make the human disagree.
- Consent-order remediation: what an issue-closure package has to prove, and
  why "we fixed it" is the least persuasive sentence in it.
- Basel III capital work — a number that moves $180M is a number nobody wants
  to challenge. The dynamic is the whole research question in miniature.
- Twenty years, three seats, one question. The regulator's seat teaches
  something the internal seat cannot, and vice versa. Worth one post, late.
