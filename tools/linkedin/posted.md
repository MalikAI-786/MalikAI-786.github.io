# Ledger

What shipped, and what it did. Filled in by hand after posting — nothing here
is collected automatically, and nothing in this repository has access to a
LinkedIn account.

The ledger exists for one reason beyond record-keeping. The operating defaults
in `.claude/skills/linkedin/references/platform.md` are labelled **conventions,
untested** — the link-in-first-comment rule, the fold budget, the hashtag
discipline. They are platform folklore. Running a feed named after evidence on
unexamined advice would be an embarrassment, so the columns below are chosen to
eventually answer them.

After roughly twenty posts there is enough here to say something. Before that,
do not read patterns into it. Six posts is noise, and the temptation to
conclude early is the exact failure the whole practice is about.

## What each column is for

| Column | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Date | Fixed Tue/Thu schedule, so timing is held constant and cannot explain a difference |
| Item | Series item (M1…) or `record` / `live` |
| Format | text / image / document / video |
| Link | `body` or `comment` — the convention under test |
| Chars | Against the 900–1,600 target and the 3,000 ceiling |
| Impressions | As reported by LinkedIn |
| Reactions · Comments · Reposts | Engagement, kept separate; comments are the signal that matters |
| Subs | Newsletter subscribers attributable to the day. Substack's own figure |
| Notes | What was actually learned. The only column that will matter in a year |

## Standing questions the ledger is answering

1. Does a link in the first comment actually outperform a link in the body?
   Alternate deliberately once there is a baseline. Do not alternate before.
2. Do Measures posts outperform working-record posts, or is the series carrying
   the feed's identity while the record posts carry its reach? Both outcomes
   change the mix.
3. Does a document post justify the production cost against a text post making
   the same argument?
4. What actually converts to a subscriber — the post, the first comment, or the
   profile visit afterward? This is the only conversion question that matters
   given what the feed is for, and it is the hardest to attribute honestly.
5. Which posts produce a speaking or teaching enquiry. Expect this to be rare,
   uncorrelated with impressions, and far more valuable than any of the above.
