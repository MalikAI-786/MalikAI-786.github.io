## 005 · week 3, Tuesday · M3 Taqlīd · the control you cannot rebuild

**The finding.** Iqbal distinguishes learning from a tradition from *taqlīd* —
servile copying of a form whose reason you have not reconstructed. His test is
whether you could rebuild it. The governance version writes itself: most mature
control environments carry controls that operate because they have always
operated, whose original risk either no longer exists or was never articulated.
Nobody removes them, because removing a control is a career risk and keeping one
is free.

**The turn.** A control you cannot derive from its risk is not a control, it is
a habit with a test script. And the population of these grows monotonically,
because control frameworks have an addition mechanism and no subtraction
mechanism.

**The open question.** What a legitimate subtraction mechanism would look like.
Rationalisation exercises exist and mostly become renaming exercises.

**Evidence needed.** Ideally one concrete, fully de-identified example of a
control whose stated rationale did not survive being asked for. He has these;
they are not in any source material I hold, and they must not touch a client,
an employer, or a specific examination. If no example can be safely told, the
post works as a general argument but lands softer.

**Blocked on:** nothing structural. Draftable now, stronger with an example.

## 007 · week 4, Tuesday · M4 Faqr · independence, stated harder

**The finding.** *Faqr* in Iqbal is not poverty and emphatically not begging. It
is indifference to what the world can withhold from you — and his claim is that
a self whose conduct is contingent on approval, comfort or a salary has already
handed someone else the controls.

That is auditor independence, stated considerably harder than the professional
standards state it. The standards address structure: reporting lines, tenure,
fee dependency. Iqbal addresses the disposition the structure exists to protect,
and notes that the structure does not produce it.

**The turn.** Every independence framework is a set of conditions under which a
person might still fail to say the thing. Structural independence is necessary
and it is not sufficient, and the profession largely stops at the necessary
part because it is the auditable part.

**The open question.** Whether disposition can be governed at all, or whether
the honest answer is that it can only be hired for — and what that implies for
how control functions recruit.

**Care required.** This post is one degree from sounding like a claim to
personal incorruptibility. It must be written about the design problem, never
about himself. If a first-person example appears, it should be one where he was
the one who did not say the thing.

**Blocked on:** nothing. Draftable now.

## 008 · week 4, Thursday · record · the instrument's blind spot

**The finding.** Iqbal published *Rumūz-i-Bekhudī* three years after the poem on
selfhood, specifically because the first had been read as a licence for a strong
individual. His correction: a self completes itself inside a community, and an
ego that has organised the world around its own development has fallen off the
ladder while facing upward.

Applied honestly, that objection lands on his own instrument. A
well-instrumented self is still a self-facing project, and no amount of careful
scoring fixes a bias sitting in the choice of what to score.

**The turn.** The governance parallel is exact and is the reason this belongs in
a professional feed: a control function optimised for its own maturity rating
has the same defect. It can be excellent by every measure it owns and still fail
the institution, because the thing it is for is not among the things it counts.

**Why this closes the first month.** It is the post that proves the series is
not self-congratulation. A reader who has watched him build an instrument for
four weeks and then watched him take it apart using its own source material will
trust the next four weeks differently. This is also the register at which he
writes best.

**Care required.** Stays self-critical throughout. The moment it becomes a
critique of other people's self-optimisation it is an ordinary post with a
citation attached.

**Blocked on:** nothing. Draftable now, and it should not be moved earlier — it
only works once 001 and 004 have established what is being criticised.
