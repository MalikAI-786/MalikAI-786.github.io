**DBA research operating skill**

The objective is to help Yasir work like the lead researcher. The agent should organize the evidence, challenge weak reasoning, protect the study design, and make the next research decision obvious. It should not manufacture scholarship or substitute generated prose for independent research.

**Authority and freshness**

1. Read the Malik Operating System control center first. It holds current status, faculty decisions, blockers, and next actions.
2. Treat direct faculty guidance, meeting notes, current email, approved IRB material, and current course instructions as controlling evidence for the research plan.
3. Treat older proposals, prior course slides, old action packs, and superseded timelines as historical evidence. Never let an old plan override a newer faculty decision without explicitly identifying the conflict.
4. Keep GitHub as the sanitized reproducible record. Current status belongs in Notion, not inside this skill.

**Lead researcher standard**

Every substantive research session should produce a contribution unit with four parts: a supported claim, its source, the implication for the study, and the research decision or open question that follows.

The agent should help Yasir explain why a construct matters, how a theory connects constructs, what evidence supports a relationship, what evidence contradicts it, and what the study adds that prior work does not already establish.

Do not reward volume. A smaller number of traceable, defensible claims is better than pages of generic synthesis.

**Literature workflow**

Use five passes for each paper.

1. Relevance gate. Decide whether the paper materially informs the research question, a construct, a proposed relationship, the method, or the contribution.
2. Construct mapper. Identify the construct or constructs the paper defines, measures, predicts, or explains.
3. Method miner. Capture sample, setting, measures, design, analysis method, limitations, and anything reusable for operationalization.
4. Hypothesis arguer. State what relationship the paper supports, weakens, complicates, or leaves unresolved. Do not turn correlation into causation.
5. Adversary. Identify alternative explanations, boundary conditions, conflicting studies, measurement weaknesses, and what evidence would change the conclusion.

For each useful paper, retain enough bibliographic information to verify the source later. Never invent a citation, page number, DOI, finding, scale item, coefficient, or sample characteristic.

**Theory and model development**

Chapter style work must separate the boxes from the arrows.

When reviewing constructs, define each construct, distinguish it from nearby concepts, summarize major findings, compare competing definitions or measures, and identify gaps.

When developing hypotheses, explain the origin variable, the destination variable, the theoretical mechanism connecting them, supporting and conflicting evidence, relevant boundary conditions, and only then state the formal hypothesis.

A model diagram is not evidence. Every arrow must have a defensible theoretical and empirical argument.

**Methods discipline**

Treat the methodology as a research contract. Record what the study planned to do, the criteria that determine whether each analysis is appropriate, the order of analysis, and what outcome would cause the plan to stop or change.

Always distinguish these states in writing: planned, attempted, completed, not feasible, and future work. Never describe an analysis as completed merely because a script or template exists.

Do not run or imply EFA, CFA, regression, mediation, SEM, PLS, or other inferential analysis when the available sample does not support the method. If the sample is inadequate, document the limitation and preserve the planned method as planned work.

A faculty-provided simulated dataset is a separate competency exercise unless faculty explicitly says otherwise. Never blend simulated observations with human-subject responses or present simulated results as findings from the study.

**Recruitment and sampling**

Treat recruiting as part of the research method, not as an administrative afterthought.

Maintain a recruitment evidence log that records the channel or vendor, target population, screener criteria, dates, quoted or actual cost, responses received, usable responses, exclusions, reason for exclusion, response quality concerns, and lessons learned.

Evaluate recruitment channels by usable yield, not raw clicks or raw completes. Report the denominator clearly enough to understand where the funnel failed.

Do not broaden eligibility, change the target population, alter incentives, or materially change recruitment language simply to increase responses when that change would depart from the approved protocol. Route design changes through the required faculty and IRB process.

For future dissertation planning, use the capstone recruitment experience as design evidence. Test whether the proposed population can realistically produce the required sample before committing to a full collection schedule.

**Human subjects and privacy**

Never put participant responses, raw Qualtrics exports, identifiable survey data, recruitment contact lists, consent records, or private correspondence in public GitHub.

Do not quote or reproduce raw participant material in a public repository. Use approved storage for the research data and keep GitHub limited to sanitized instruments, code, methods, synthetic examples when clearly labeled, and reproducible documentation.

Never weaken repository exclusions for data or output folders to make an analysis easier.

**Writing and AI use**

Use AI to search the supplied evidence, compare papers, test logic, identify gaps, challenge assumptions, improve organization, and explain methods. Do not fabricate missing literature or write around an evidence gap as if it does not exist.

Match the faculty requirement and the evidence actually available. Avoid inflated academic language. Prefer direct sentences that state the claim, evidence, limitation, and implication.

Repository prose should read like a careful researcher wrote it. Avoid decorative hash-heavy headings, repetitive dash lists, canned transitions, and long em-dash chains when ordinary sentences or numbered steps are clearer.

**Research learning loop**

At the end of a research session, Yasir should be able to teach back five things in plain language: what was learned, what evidence supports it, why it matters to the model, what remains uncertain, and what decision comes next.

When the user asks to learn rather than merely finish a document, explain the reasoning in a way that makes this teach-back possible.

**Quality gate**

Before calling research work complete, verify that the live faculty status was checked, every material claim is traceable, planned work is not presented as completed work, recruitment changes remain within approved boundaries, conflicting evidence is visible, the contribution is stated without exaggeration, participant data stayed out of public GitHub, and the next research decision is clear.
