# Merch — the hoodie and what comes after

## Why the bar is high

Cotton cannot be edited. A misattributed line on a garment is permanent, it is
photographed, and it is worn by someone who trusted the account. For a page
whose entire positioning is *we get this right*, one wrong hoodie costs more
than a year of correct posts earns.

So the gates below are not brand fussiness. They are the same control logic the
rest of this repo runs on: an irreversible action gets a stricter test than a
reversible one.

## The three gates

A line reaches print only if it clears all three.

**Gate 1 — Provenance verified.** Tier A in `iqbal-corpus.md`, checked against
a printed source, collection named. Tier B does not qualify no matter how good
it looks. This gate is absolute and has no exceptions, including for a line
that is obviously his.

**Gate 2 — Proven in the feed.** It has already beaten the account's median on
**saves ÷ reach**, not likes. Saves measure "I want to keep this", which is the
nearest free signal to "I would pay to wear this". Shipping a line the audience
has never responded to is guessing with inventory.

**Gate 3 — Legible out of context.** It has to work on a stranger who does not
follow the account and may not read Urdu. The test: *on a train, to someone
who has never heard of Iqbal, does this read as something — or as decoration?*
This gate is what argues for very short Urdu with small roman support, or a
clean English line, and against printing a full couplet, which is a caption
wearing a hoodie.

## Brand constraint

The identity system already exists in this repo and merch inherits it rather
than inventing alongside it — that is the point of having one. Tokens and the
seven rules live in `brand.html` and the control center. The two that bite on
apparel:

- **Ember `#E0662E` is for marks, rules and display type — never body text on
  light.** It measures 3.11:1 on paper. A hoodie print is display type, so
  ember is correct for the line itself and wrong for any small print.
- **One ember element per view.** A garment is one view. One ember thing.

Warm neutrals only, no cold greys — which rules out the default heather grey
most blank suppliers push, and points at bone, warm black, or ember on warm
black.

## Candidates, ranked

**1 · خودی** — one word. Maximum legibility, needs no translation for the
in-group, and it *is* the account's concept and half its name. Gate 1 trivially
clear. Gate 3 stronger than anything else available, because a single word
reads as a mark rather than a message. Small roman `khudī` beneath, or nothing.

**2 · یقین · عمل · محبت** *(yaqīn · ʿamal · moḥabbat)* — the three faculties,
from the Bāl-e-Jibrīl couplet. Triadic, sits naturally across a chest, and it
is the same spine the Mīzān instrument scores — so the garment and the practice
say the same thing, which is the sort of coherence people can feel without
being able to name. Gate 1 clear.

**3 · تو شاہیں ہے** *(tū shāhīn hai — "you are a falcon")* — short, verbal,
carries an image. Gate 1 clear. Pairs with the *Falcon file* series, so the
feed does the selling before the product exists. Watch Gate 3: it is a second-
person address, which is either arresting or slightly odd on a stranger — worth
testing as a print before a run.

**Not yet:** the full "Khudī ko kar buland" couplet. It is the strongest line in
the corpus and the weakest garment — two hemistiches of Urdu is a paragraph on
a chest. It belongs on the inside label, or on the neck, or nowhere.

## Sequence

Do not open a store. Print one design, small run, and let the feed sell it —
the *Falcon file* and *Khudī, applied* series are the marketing, and they should
run for weeks before anything is for sale. The account earns the right to sell
something by first being worth following without it.
