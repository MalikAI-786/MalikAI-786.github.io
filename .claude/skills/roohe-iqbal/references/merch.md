# Merch — irreversible publication gate

A garment is harder to correct than a post, so it needs stricter controls.

## Three gates
1. **Provenance verified.** Tier A in `iqbal-corpus.md`, collection named and checked against a reliable edition.
2. **Proven in the feed.** The line has beaten the account median on saves ÷ reach; do not guess with inventory.
3. **Legible out of context.** It must work for a stranger who may not know Iqbal or read Urdu.

## Brand constraint
Merch inherits the existing Reference Mark identity. Use canonical tokens and assets; do not invent a parallel brand. Ember is a display accent, not small body text. Warm neutrals are preferred over cold grey defaults.

## Candidate logic
Short, source-secure lines or concepts outperform paragraph-length couplets on garments. `خودی`, `یقین · عمل · محبت`, and short shāhīn language are structurally stronger candidates than a full two-line poem.

## Sequence
Do not start with a store. Test the concept in the feed, prove audience retention/saves, then run a small reversible batch before scaling.
