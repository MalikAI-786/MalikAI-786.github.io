# The content system

## Five-beat reel grammar
1. **Contradiction (0–1.5s):** a claim that collides with expectation; not the couplet.
2. **The line (1.5–4s):** Urdu/Persian on screen, one hemistich at a time.
3. **Plain reading (4–9s):** ordinary English translation.
4. **The turn (9–20s):** what it actually means or how common usage misses it.
5. **The source (last ~2s):** collection on screen every time.

Hooks should put something at risk. Generic “Iqbal said…” openings are too weak.

## Five series
- **Misquoted** — wrong attribution or inverted readings; requires strongest provenance discipline.
- **Khudī, applied** — one force per episode from the khudī ladder.
- **He said it first** — Iqbal anticipating modern problems such as outsourcing judgment and imitation.
- **The falcon file** — unpack the shāhīn one attribute at a time.
- **Two lines, one argument** — short-form cadence format.

## Caption structure
Argument first, then original couplet, transliteration, plain-English translation, collection, one answerable question, then a restrained hashtag set.

## Metrics
Prioritize saves ÷ reach, shares ÷ reach, 3-second retention, source-related comments and follows per post. Likes are secondary.

## Cadence
Batch production. Prefer a sustainable multi-post weekly session over a fragile daily-start habit.
