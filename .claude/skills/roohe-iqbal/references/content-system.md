# The content system

## Format grammar — the five beats

This genre has about 1.5 seconds before the thumb moves. The beats below are
ordered by what the viewer needs, not by what is satisfying to make.

| Beat | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **1. The contradiction** | 0–1.5s | A claim that collides with what the viewer already believes. **Not the couplet.** |
| **2. The line** | 1.5–4s | The Urdu, on screen, one hemistich at a time. Let it breathe; this is the beat people screenshot. |
| **3. The plain reading** | 4–9s | Translation in ordinary English. No ornament — ornament here reads as evasion. |
| **4. The turn** | 9–20s | What it actually means, or the gap between how it is used and what it says. **This is the whole post.** |
| **5. The source** | last 2s | Collection, on screen, every time. It is the brand mark. |

The hook is where these live or die. Openers that work in this niche share one
property: they put something *at risk*.

- "This is the most quoted line in Urdu poetry. Almost everyone has it backwards."
- "Iqbal did not write this."
- "He wrote a second book because people misread the first one."
- "Your grandfather quoted this at weddings. It is about failure."

Openers that do not work: "Iqbal said…", "Beautiful poetry by Allama Iqbal",
"Today's couplet." They are true and they are invisible.

## The five series

A page compounds through series and dissolves through one-offs. Every post
should be an episode of something, and the series should be visible on the grid.

**1 · Misquoted** — lines attributed wrongly, or read as their own opposite.
Highest share rate in the genre because being corrected is interesting and
correcting others is more so. The one that carries risk: **you must be right.**
Every episode needs its source verified twice before it ships.

**2 · Khudī, applied** — one force per episode, from the ladder: ʿishq, faqr,
himmat, kasb-e-ḥalāl, taskhīr on the upward side; su'āl, taqlīd, yās, ghulāmī,
the trance on the downward. This is the spine series and it maps exactly onto
the material already written on the Mīzān khudī page — the arguments are done,
they need cutting to length. Roughly ten episodes sitting there already.

**3 · He said it first** — Iqbal anticipating a modern problem. The self versus
mechanism; imitation (*taqlīd*) versus reconstruction; a civilisation that
outsources its judgment. **This is the only series nobody else in the niche can
run credibly**, because it needs someone who actually works on machine-assisted
judgment. It is also the series that connects the account to the professional
identity without either one embarrassing the other.

**4 · The falcon file** — the *shāhīn* unpacked one attribute at a time: does
not scavenge, does not nest in the palace, keeps altitude, lives on the rock.
Visually native and merch-native; this is where prints come from.

**5 · Two lines, one argument** — the short form, for cadence. A couplet and a
single sentence of reading. Fifteen seconds. Exists so the week can be filled
without lowering the standard of the other four.

## Captions

The video carries the couplet; the caption carries the argument and does the
search work.

```
[One sentence: the argument, stated flat. Not the couplet.]

[Urdu couplet, both lines]
[Roman transliteration]
"[Plain English translation]"
— [Collection]

[One question a stranger can answer in five words.]

[5–7 fixed tags + 2 rotating]
```

Why this order: the first line is all most people see before "more", so it
cannot be the couplet they already scrolled past in the video. The
transliteration and translation exist because they are the only searchable text
on a video post and because a serious share of the audience reads Urdu by ear,
not by script. The question exists because comments are a ranking signal and an
open-ended "thoughts?" gets nothing — answerable questions get replies.

Never thirty hashtags. It reads as a page that is buying reach rather than
earning it, which is precisely the wrong signal for this positioning.

## What to measure

Ignore likes. They are the cheapest available action and correlate with nothing
that matters here.

| Metric | Why |
|---|---|
| **Saves ÷ reach** | Did anyone think it was worth keeping? The closest available proxy for whether a line could sell as an object. |
| **Shares ÷ reach** | Did it survive being passed to someone with no context? The merch test in miniature. |
| **3s retention** | Grades the hook alone. If this is weak the rest of the post was never seen. |
| **Comment questions** | People asking about the *source* means the positioning is landing. |
| **Follows per post** | The only metric that compounds. |

Keep the numbers in the decode ledger so the pattern is visible across posts
rather than re-argued each time. Three posts is a mood; twenty is a finding.

## Cadence

Four a week, batched in one session. The constraint is real and worth designing
around rather than pretending away: this runs alongside a full-time examiner
role and a doctorate, and the failure mode is not a bad post, it is three weeks
of silence after an ambitious start.

One Sunday: shoot four, cut four, write four captions, schedule. The batching
matters more than the count — every separate act of starting costs more than
the work itself.
