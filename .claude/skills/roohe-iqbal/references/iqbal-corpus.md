# Corpus register

Three tiers. **Nothing moves up a tier without being checked against a printed
kulliyāt or a scholarly edition** — not a search result, not another Instagram
page, not this file. This file records the state of checking; it is not itself
the authority.

The account's positioning is accuracy. That makes this register the most
load-bearing file in the skill.

**Structural claims, also Tier A** (paraphrase, never in quotation marks):

- The three stages in the education of khudī — *iṭāʿat*, *ẓabt-e-nafs*,
  *niyābat-e-ilāhī* — are the architecture of **Asrār-i-Khudī** (1915, Persian).
- **Rumūz-i-Bekhudī** (1918, Persian) is the communal correction, written
  because the first poem was read as a licence for the strong individual.
- The ego as directive energy, admitting of degrees and capable of decay;
  destiny as the reach of a thing's possibilities; serial time versus duration
  — all **The Reconstruction of Religious Thought in Islam** (1930), in prose,
  in English.

## Working rules

1. **Virality is evidence against accuracy, not for it.** The most-shared
   version of a couplet is the most likely to be corrupted — lines get smoothed
   for quotability as they travel.
2. **Persian is not Urdu.** *Asrār*, *Rumūz*, *Payām-e-Mashriq* and *Javīd
   Nāma* are Persian. Quoting them in Urdu transliteration without saying so is
   an error this audience catches.
3. **Nicholson's *The Secrets of the Self*** is the standard English *Asrār*
   and carries a useful introduction; it is a translation, so quote it as
   Nicholson rendering Iqbal, not as Iqbal.
4. **Two editions differ.** Iqbal revised — most famously softening the attack
   on Ḥāfiẓ in later editions of *Asrār* after the controversy it caused. When
   a line is contested, which edition is being quoted is a real question and
   sometimes the whole story.
5. When a check fails, **move the entry to Tier C with the reason** rather than
   deleting it. The record of what was rejected is worth as much as the record
   of what was kept, and it is what stops the same wrong line being
   re-discovered in six months.
