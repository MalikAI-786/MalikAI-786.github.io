# Citation register

Every source claim currently on the page, with its reference and provenance
class. Check here before re-quoting; add here when you add one.

**Provenance classes**

- **A — verifiable reference.** Qur'an by sūrah:āyah, ḥadīth by collection.
  The reader can go behind it. Arabic on the page is set for reading, not as a
  recitation reference; verify against a muṣḥaf before any public use.
- **B — attributed quotation.** Iqbal, with collection where the attribution
  is established.
- **C — paraphrase.** Someone's argument restated, labelled as restatement.
  Never presented as quotation.
- **D — constructed.** Built for this instrument. No authority beyond
  usefulness. Must be declared as such on the page.
- **E — empirical direction.** Reflects the general direction of a research
  literature, stated without citation, flagged as a prompt to verify. Never
  invent a study, author, or figure to support one of these.

## Fiqh position

The majority position treats intoxicating or mind-clouding substances —
cannabis included in the classical discussions from Ibn Taymiyyah onward —
under the ruling for khamr. The page **states this and explicitly declines to
issue a ruling**, directing the question to a scholar. Preserve that framing
exactly. Do not soften it into approval and do not harden it into a fatwa.
